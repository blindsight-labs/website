Blindsight Sub-processors

Version 1.0 · Effective 19 December 2026

blindsight.io/platform/legal/subprocessors

This document covers the Blindsight platform (the console, the desktop apps and the APIs). The blindsight.io website has its own privacy notice and terms (the website has no separate terms page; its privacy policy is the only website legal document). All platform legal documents are listed in the platform legal index.

1. About this list

Blindsight Technologies AG, Rennweg 57, 8001 Zurich, Switzerland (UID CHE-484.046.837) ("Blindsight") provides a desktop agent and a hosted platform. When a customer ("Customer") uses the product, Blindsight processes Customer Personal Data on the Customer's behalf and acts as processor under the data processing agreement at https://blindsight.io/platform/legal/dpa (the "DPA"; clause 10.1 of the Blindsight Endpoint Licence Agreement). To do so, Blindsight uses the providers listed in Table A. These are the sub-processors under the DPA, and Annex 3 of the DPA lists the same providers.

This page also lists, for transparency, services a Customer may choose to connect (Table B), providers that support the product but do not receive Customer Personal Data (Table C), and providers Blindsight uses as controller for its own business (section 5), which are described in the privacy notice at https://blindsight.io/platform/legal/privacy.

Notice of changes. Blindsight will tell the Customer's administrators by e-mail, and will update this page, at least 30 days before a new sub-processor starts to process Customer Personal Data, or before an existing one is given a materially wider scope or a new location (DPA clause 6.2). The Customer may object within that period as DPA clause 6.3 provides.

5. Providers used by Blindsight as controller

These providers process personal data for Blindsight's own purposes (billing, licensing and support). They are described in the Blindsight privacy notice, not here. One line each:

  • Stripe (Stripe Payments Europe, Ltd. and Stripe, Inc.): card and invoice payments for self-serve subscriptions, through a separate licence console; Stripe collects payer name, billing address, tax identifier and payment details at checkout, and acts as a separate controller for payment processing under its own terms.
  • Keygen, United States (Virginia): licence records, including the Customer's billing contact name and e-mail address where Blindsight creates a Keygen user for the account.
  • Amazon Web Services EMEA SARL, eu-west-1: hosting of the licence console (one virtual server in Ireland) and its database and backups.
  • Fonts: the platform console and the licence console serve their fonts from Blindsight's own servers, so no font request goes to a third party.
  • Google reCAPTCHA Enterprise: not used. Bot protection on the console sign-in page is disabled in production, so no sign-in data goes to Google for this purpose.
  • Providers used for the blindsight.io website (hosting, forms, analytics, consent and mail) are listed in the website's own privacy notice at https://blindsight.io/privacy.
