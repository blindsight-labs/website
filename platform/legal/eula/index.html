8. Operation of the Software and the Customer's responsibilities

8.1 What the Software does. To protect data sent to AI services, the Software, once installed and enrolled in the Service:(a) creates a root certificate on each device, adds it to the device's certificate trust stores, and uses it to decrypt and inspect encrypted traffic to the AI services selected by the Customer's policy in the Service; it does not inspect the content of sign-in and identity pages;(b) depending on the operating system and the Customer's settings, directs that traffic through a local proxy, a virtual network adapter or a packet filter driver installed on the device;(c) detects personal data and other sensitive information in that traffic on the device and, according to the Customer's policy, allows, flags, redacts, pseudonymises or blocks it;(d) sends to the Service identifiers of the device and of its user, status information and records of its findings, including the names of files submitted to AI services, and, where the Customer's policy allows text to leave the device (which is the default setting), the text of prompts, replies, system instructions, tool results and files submitted to AI services, after redaction on the device; the Service analyses that text for security threats, sensitive information and the Customer's rules, as described in the DPA; and(e) checks for and installs updates under clause 9.The Documentation describes these functions and the settings that control them.

8.2 The Customer's responsibilities. The Customer decides whether, where and how to deploy the Software and how to configure the Service, and is responsible for:(a) having a lawful basis under applicable data protection law for the processing of personal data by means of the Software and the Service;(b) informing Authorised Users and other persons concerned, before the Software is used on their devices, in a clear and accessible way, of what the Software inspects, what is recorded and who can see it;(c) informing and consulting, or obtaining the agreement of, works councils, staff representatives, trade unions or authorities where applicable law or collective agreements require it;(d) carrying out any data protection impact assessment that applicable law requires;(e) its policy settings in the Service, including which services are inspected, how identities are displayed, how long data is kept and whether text may leave devices; and(f) its rules on the private use of devices and AI services.Blindsight provides the Documentation and reasonable assistance as set out in the DPA.

8.3 Intended purpose. The Software and the Service are intended to protect personal data, confidential information and the security of the Customer's systems when AI services are used. They are not designed or intended to evaluate the performance, productivity or behaviour of individuals, to infer emotions, or to take or support decisions on recruitment, promotion, termination, task allocation or other terms of work. The Customer shall not use them for those purposes. Detection uses rules and statistical models; it will not detect every item of sensitive information and may flag information that is not sensitive.

8.4 Acceptable use. The Customer shall not use the Software (a) on devices that it does not own or control, or to inspect the traffic of persons who are not Authorised Users, (b) to intercept communications or monitor individuals in breach of applicable law, or (c) to use the device root certificate or its private key for any purpose other than the operation of the Software.
